## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of this project is to build a high-caliber, fully responsive business website for a local boutique (**Elegance Boutique**). The website is engineered to serve as a **real live commercial web solution** that can be presented directly to a boutique owner to digitize their local business, showcase their craftsmanship, and drive customer inquiries.

---

## 💡 Business Problem & Challenges

Local fashion boutiques face several operational and marketing challenges in today's digital landscape:

1. **Hidden Inventory & Catalog Limits:** Customers walking by may not be aware of the full variety of sarees, custom blouses, lehengas, or tailored wear available inside the shop.
2. **Low Online Visibility:** Potential clients searching for "saree boutique near me" or "custom blouse stitching" cannot easily discover the business online.
3. **Missed Phone Inquiries:** During peak store hours, boutique owners miss phone calls from customers asking about pricing, opening hours, or custom tailoring timelines.
4. **Communication of Special Services:** Unique services like private bridal consultations or custom Maggam embroidery tailoring are hard to showcase without a dedicated web platform.
5. **Lack of Location & Hours Clarity:** Customers frequently struggle to find exact store locations, directions, or holiday opening hours.

---

## 🚀 Business Solution & Impact

This website addresses these local business challenges effectively:

* **24/7 Digital Showroom:** Presents complete product categories (Sarees, Blouses, Lehengas, Kurtis, Ethnic Wear, Party Wear) with high-definition imagery and custom detail tags.
* **Streamlined Customer Enquiry Engine:** Interactive form with category selection, date picker, and input validation, plus instant 1-click WhatsApp enquiry buttons.
* **SEO-Optimized Local Discovery:** Semantic HTML5, structured Open Graph meta tags, and local search optimization so nearby customers find the shop easily.
* **Clear Location & Visiting Details:** Dedicated contact section featuring interactive Google Maps navigation, phone numbers, and real-time open/closed indicators.
* **Modular & Easy to Customize:** Formatted so any boutique owner can quickly update store names, phone numbers, addresses, product items, and prices in simple HTML.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Sticky Navigation Bar
* Sticky header with blur backdrop and elevation shadow on scroll.
* Smooth scrolling links to all key page sections.
* Mobile-responsive drawer menu with smooth hamburger transition and accessibility ARIA attributes.

### 2. Visually Impressive Hero Section
* Headline: *"Elegance That Defines You"*
* High-definition ethnic fashion banner with dark rose overlay gradient for maximum text contrast.
* Primary & secondary Call to Action (CTA) buttons: *"Explore Collection"* & *"Enquire Now"*.

### 3. About The Boutique
* Focused narrative introducing timeless ethnic styles and modern Indian fashion.
* Realistic business claims without unverified awards or figures.
* Highlight cards detailing customer-focused approach and fashion styling focus.

### 4. Categorized Collections & Featured Styles
* 6 major product category cards with gold border accents and hover zoom effects.
* **Featured Styles Showcase:** Includes Silk Sarees, Embroidered Blouses, Festive Lehengas, Designer Kurtis, Party Wear, and Bridal Trousseau.
* Clear sample pricing badges (`Price on Enquiry` & sample estimates).

### 5. Services & "Why Choose Us"
* 6 core services (Custom Blouse Design, Custom Tailoring, Alterations, Styling Assistance, Bridal Wear Consultation, Occasion Wear Selection) with custom SVG icons.
* 6 value proposition cards explaining the boutique's quality commitment.

### 6. Special Offers Showcase
* Highlights for Festive Collections and Bridal Trousseau consultations, clearly labeled as sample boutique showcases.

### 7. Interactive Gallery with Lightbox
* Responsive grid showcasing showroom interiors, saree racks, stitching atelier, and embroidery details.
* Dynamic category filtering buttons (`All`, `Sarees`, `Blouses`, `Lehengas`, `Boutique Interior`).
* Fullscreen interactive image Lightbox modal with next/prev buttons and keyboard (`Escape`, `Arrow keys`) support.

### 8. Interactive Customer Enquiry Form
* Fields: Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Interested Category dropdown, Preferred Date, and Detailed Message.
* Client-side JavaScript validation with real-time error hints.
* Demo submission handler showing an elegant success alert: *"Thank you! Your enquiry has been received. The boutique will contact you soon."*
* Auto-prefill helper when clicking *"Enquire Now"* on any product card.

### 9. Contact & Social Integration
* Demo contact details (Address, Phone, Email, Timings, Status Badge).
* WhatsApp instant messaging integration (`https://wa.me/...`).
* Google Maps location placeholder with *"Get Directions"*.
* Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, WhatsApp).

### 10. Dedicated Business Value Section
* Explicit section titled *"How Our Website Helps Your Business"* demonstrating tangible ROI for local shop owners.

### 11. Interactive FAQ Accordion
* Accessible collapsible accordion answering common questions on appointments, stitching timelines, and customizations.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **HTML5:** Semantic document structure, ARIA accessibility attributes, form controls.
* **CSS3:** Flexbox, CSS Grid, Custom Properties (variables), keyframe animations, media queries.
* **JavaScript (Vanilla ES6+):** DOM manipulation, IntersectionObserver API, event delegation, form validation, modal state management.
* **SVG Vector Graphics:** High-resolution local offline vector fallbacks in `images/`.
* **No Framework Dependencies:** Zero external heavy JS frameworks (React/Vue/jQuery) or CSS libraries for maximum loading speed and beginner-friendly code clarity.

## 💻 How to Run Locally

1. **Clone or Download the Repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Dishavaishnavi-design/Village-Welfare-Alert-Reminder-System.git
   cd project
   ```
2. **Open `index.html` in any web browser:**
   * Double-click `index.html` directly, OR
   * Use VS Code extension **Live Server** to view with hot reload.
3. **Test Features:**
   * Resize your window to check mobile/tablet responsiveness.
   * Try clicking on the gallery items to test the Lightbox.
   * Fill out the Enquiry Form to test client-side validation and success alert.
   * Test the product "Enquire Now" auto-fill buttons.

## 🌐 How to Deploy Live

### Option A: GitHub Pages (Free & Fast)
1. Push all project files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `README.md`, `images/`) to your GitHub repository.
2. Navigate to your repository **Settings** -> **Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and `/ (root)` folder.
4. Click **Save**. Your site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

### Option B: Netlify / Vercel
1. Drag and drop the project folder directly into the Netlify/Vercel dashboard.
2. Deploy instantly with a custom domain or free preview link.
